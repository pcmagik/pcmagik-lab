About

The method

One measurement at a time.

PC Magik Lab exists because "it depends" is not a number. Here, it depends on a specific model, a specific card, and a specific prompt — and we measure exactly that.

"Every episode is one measurement with one changed variable and one number on screen."

PC Magik (Mateusz Piekut) is a computer service and homelab operator from Poland. The lab is his rack; the GPU is his; the measurements are yours to reproduce. Prompts, configs, and raw outputs for every run are committed to the open repository.
