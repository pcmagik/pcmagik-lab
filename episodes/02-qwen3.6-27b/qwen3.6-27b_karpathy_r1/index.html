About

About the series

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series dedicated to running AI models on real, local GPUs — with full transparency and honest benchmarks.

Each episode puts models through the same rigorous testing process: identical prompts, identical hardware, and a full model reload before every run.

The goal is simple — give you trustworthy, comparable data about how AI models actually perform on consumer-grade hardware.

All prompts, configurations, and result files are published openly so anyone can verify the results.
