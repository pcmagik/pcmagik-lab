About PC Magik Lab

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series dedicated to testing AI models on real, local hardware. We believe in transparency, reproducibility, and showing the true capabilities of AI models when running on your own hardware.

Every test is conducted with the same methodology, same hardware, and same prompts. We publish everything so you can verify our results and reproduce our experiments.

Join us as we explore the world of local AI and discover what your GPU can really do.
