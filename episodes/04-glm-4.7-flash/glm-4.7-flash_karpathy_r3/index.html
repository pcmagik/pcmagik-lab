About PC Magik

PC Magik is a YouTube series dedicated to testing AI models on real hardware. The goal is simple: show what actually happens when you run AI models locally.

Created by Mateusz Piekut, a computer service and homelab operator from Poland. The series focuses on transparency, consistency, and real-world performance.

Every test follows strict methodology: same prompts, same rig, one variable changed per test. All results are published for verification.
