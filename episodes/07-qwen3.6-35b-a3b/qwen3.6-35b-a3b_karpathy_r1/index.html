About

The series.

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series dedicated to running local AI models on real, consumer-grade GPUs. No cloud instances, no lab servers — just a real desktop pushing real models on real hardware.

Each episode tests a different model or configuration, measuring performance in a transparent, reproducible way. Every prompt and every result file is published openly so anyone can verify the numbers.

The goal is simple: help you understand what your own GPU can do with local AI, and make informed choices about which models work for your setup.
