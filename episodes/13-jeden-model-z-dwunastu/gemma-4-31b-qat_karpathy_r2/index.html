Methodology

Consistent Prompts

Every test uses the exact same prompt to ensure apples-to-apples comparisons.

Fixed Rig

Tests are performed on the same hardware rig to eliminate environment variables.

Single Variable

Only one variable is changed per test to isolate the impact of specific changes.

Fresh State

The model is reloaded before every single run to ensure no state leakage.

Open Data

Every prompt and result file is published for full transparency and verification.

About

PC Magik Lab is a dedicated exploration of artificial intelligence performance in a local environment. The series focuses on the practical realities of running LLMs on consumer-grade hardware, moving beyond theoretical benchmarks to real-world execution.

By utilizing local GPUs and open-source tools, the lab aims to provide a clear, verifiable look at how different models and configurations perform under controlled conditions.
