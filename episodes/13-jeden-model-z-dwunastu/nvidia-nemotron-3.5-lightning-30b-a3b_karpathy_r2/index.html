About

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series created by Mateusz Piekut, a computer service technician and homelab operator based in Poland. The series explores the practical side of running AI models on local hardware, giving viewers real-world insights without cloud dependencies.

All content is driven by curiosity, rigorous testing, and the belief that anyone should be able to run powerful models on their own gear.
