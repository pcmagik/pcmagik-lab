About

Running AI where it matters — on your own hardware.

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series about running AI models on real, local GPUs. The focus is on practical, repeatable testing instead of hype — the same prompt, the same rig, one variable changed at a time.

Every prompt and every result file is published so viewers can verify the numbers themselves. No cloud, no black boxes — just models running on real hardware you could own.
