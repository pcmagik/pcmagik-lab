How It Works

About

PC Magik Lab is a YouTube series created by Mateusz Piekut, a computer service technician and homelab operator based in Poland. The series explores the practical side of running artificial intelligence models on real, locally-installed GPUs.

By keeping the testing environment consistent—same prompt, same rig, one variable changed per test—the series provides clear, reproducible insights into how different models perform on consumer-grade hardware.

All episodes, prompts, and result files are published openly on the YouTube channel and associated repository, ensuring transparency and community access to the data.
